# New visual work

Use this flow for a new surface or a replacement visual identity. PRODUCT.md owns product truth, DESIGN.md owns durable visual decisions, and a surface brief keeps one route's strategy. The engine prints the rest at the moment it applies (concept-seed's MODE RULES and PRESENTATION blocks, serve-question's ANSWER and NEXT lines, build-phase's NEXT lines and gate reasons): each is an instruction, followed when it prints. This file holds only what they do not say.

## 1. Decide what is already true

Read DESIGN.md, representative code, tokens, components, and assets. **Redesign:** keep product truth, content, function, constraints, and brand commitments; replace the old visual world, whose look is evidence, never authority. **Established world:** inherit it, documented or not. **Incomplete brand:** keep confirmed assets and traits, expand with the user. **No visual authority:** create a world with the user. A section, component, or state inside an established surface inherits that surface.

## 2. Ask what will change the work

Before implementation, ask two or three related questions through the structured question tool; a precise request needs only a compact confirmation, never none. Persuade: who must act, what they should believe, which real proof earns it. Operate: the task, information, states, frequency, constraints. Read: the reader's question, sources, structure, wayfinding. Experience: what leads, how exploration unfolds, which transition matters. In every mode: what success looks like, what must stay untouched, what would make a polished result feel wrong. Never ask for CSS values or aesthetic lanes.

## 3. Choose the right amount of invention

### Extend an existing surface

Inherit its world and composition; resolve only the new content, hierarchy, states, and interaction. No concept round, no DESIGN.md change unless the user approves a durable system change.

### Create a whole surface inside an established world

Derive five to seven materially different structures from the content and task, ordered by resonance, then run `{{scripts_path}}/impeccable concept-seed --scope surface --mode <mode>` and follow what it prints. Anchor every card's comp on a screenshot of a representative existing page passed as a reference image. A locked comp is the approved comp: start the build with `build-phase start --comp <that file>` and skip visualize.md's comp round. A locked wireframe builds code-led. Never roll for a local extension or a precise narrow request.

### Create or replace the visual world

1. **Ground.** Name the product's unique mechanism in one sentence, the audience's real scene, its cultural home, and what this first surface must prove. The page this category always ships and its predictable opposite are the rut; so is the literal reading of a brief that paints its own picture (spend at most one candidate on it).
2. **List seven.** Concrete visual systems, artifacts, places, or rituals the audience knows by heart, including its graphic and screen traditions, each with one line on why it carries the mechanism, ordered by resonance. Ask what this would be as a physical object and what its world looked like before the web. On Persuade and Experience, the audience's own tools count toward the rut (at most one). Near-duplicates count once; span at least three material families.
3. **Roll.** Run `{{scripts_path}}/impeccable concept-seed --scope direction --mode <mode>` and follow what it prints. No substitute, no skip: writing artifact code on a new or replacement world before the roll has run and its assignment is acknowledged is a contract violation, whatever the harness, model, or time pressure. Fuse, judge, and raise exactly as it prints; a lifted motif is a costume note, never a raise. <!-- rule:skill-concept-procedure --> <!-- rule:skill-verdict-and-donation -->
4. **Present.** One direction, fully committed and raised, each raise a named line; winning and competitive challengers as full alternates, at most three, the rest noted for the re-roll pool; declined challengers demoted with their kept line; the pick card only as the seed allows; re-roll with steer and its safer and bolder registers, which are the user's to choose. "Bolder" or "safer" said during an open round means these registers, never the commands. <!-- rule:skill-pick-card-one-only -->
5. **Standing exit.** The category standard, played straight, sits on every direction round as the user's door, never yours: never recommend it, weigh it against the roll, or let it soften the dealt hand. When taken, the CANON CHOSEN line governs, and a standing preference becomes a brand commitment in PRODUCT.md. <!-- rule:skill-canon-standing-exit -->
6. **Re-roll and pins.** A re-roll eliminates everything shown; after two in a row, ask what quality is missing. Re-roll on your own only when the assignment cannot carry the product's truth or task; taste is never grounds. A user- or brief-pinned direction beats the roll; where only the assignment's materials conflict with a pin or a brand commitment, translate them and name the translation. <!-- rule:skill-assigned-plus-reroll -->

**The decision page** is the channel: concept-seed closes every roll with a PRESENTATION block that carries its procedure and names the recorded build path; follow it. On a reroll ANSWER, rerun concept-seed with the same `--scope` and `--mode` plus `--from <seed-key> --reroll <n>` and deliver the hand with `--update` on the same key. A harness that can leave a shell blocked in the background may run serve-question without `--start` and let it block. One that cannot hold a blocking `--wait` takes the structured tool without starting the page and says so in the first reply; a shell that hands back a still-running command as a session you can poll holds the wait, and you rerun `--wait` only after it exits without an answer. Only exit code 2 from starting it, or that wait check before starting, sends the round to the structured tool, where declined challengers fold into the assigned option as kept lines. <!-- rule:skill-visual-decision-page -->

**Decision comps.** When serve-question prints `NEXT read .../visualize.md`, read it before the first prompt. With parallel subagents, fan out one shipped asset producer per card, up to four in flight, each given that card's fields, PRODUCT.md, the frame, visualize.md's path, the MODE RULES COMPS section verbatim, and the declared path; regenerate inline any slot empty on return, drop any slot empty when the user answers. Without image generation, palette chips and facts make a complete page. <!-- rule:skill-decision-comps-full-fidelity --> <!-- rule:skill-salience-parity -->

**Build path.** Comp-led or code-led is a recorded workflow preference, never a round's question; PRESENTATION names it. When `buildPathFlipped` returns true on a project with no recorded `buildPath`, ask once after the round whether to keep it: yes writes the flipped value to `.impeccable/config.json`, "just this once" writes the value they flipped away from. A user asking in words to change it gets the file updated without a question. Code-led is not a discount: its ambition moves into the FIRST VIEWPORT block and a named signature interaction and motion grammar. <!-- rule:skill-build-path-round -->

**Truth.** Every direction the roll can land on is already viable: every claim it visualizes true, a real palette and component family, one product-specific experience, workable with the available assets and budget; one that fails on truth is replaced before the roll. Truth binds claims, not demonstrations: author illustrative material at full fidelity, label it synthetic where a visitor could mistake it, and hand the user the replace list. Prices, customers, benchmarks, endpoints, and capabilities stay uninventable. Refusing a bold direction because its demo data does not exist yet is timidity wearing honesty's clothes. <!-- rule:skill-truth-binds-claims -->

**Mode.** The MODE RULES block governs every direction, card, and comp and wins over shared guidance. With no roll (a pinned direction), read [mode-persuade.md](mode-persuade.md) for Persuade and Experience, [mode-operate.md](mode-operate.md), or [mode-read.md](mode-read.md).

## 4. Commit the world

- **Color.** Pick a strategy before colors: Restrained (tinted neutrals plus one accent), Committed (one saturated color over 30-60% of the surface), Full palette (3-4 named roles), or Drenched (the surface is the color). Persuade and Experience may take the bolder ones. Color commits at page scale, fields owning whole regions. <!-- rule:skill-color-strategy -->
- **Faces.** Chosen like objects from the subject's world. Operate and Read are well served by system stacks and workhorse UI faces. On Persuade and Experience these defaults mean you stopped looking: Fraunces, Playfair Display, Cormorant, Lora, Crimson, Newsreader, Syne, Space Grotesk, Space Mono, IBM Plex, Inter-as-display, DM Sans, DM Serif, Outfit, Plus Jakarta Sans, Instrument Sans; one needs a reason no other face satisfies, and a subject association (books want a serif, tech wants a mono) is never it. <!-- rule:skill-typo-reflex-faces -->
- **Calibration.** AI interfaces cluster in four looks: cream ground, serif display, terracotta or signal-red accent; near-black with one neon accent and glow; editorial hairlines, italic serif, tracked mono labels; flat saturated ink with black boxes and hard offset shadows. Legitimate when the brief asks; where the aesthetic is free, landing in one, or being guessable from the category, fails the self-check. <!-- rule:skill-calibration-saturated-looks --> Negative constraints (no hype) rule out devices, not energy. <!-- rule:skill-constraints-rule-out-devices-not-energy --> A bookish, warm, or child-facing subject spans the whole saturated spectrum of cloth, thread, and jackets; cream plus serif is the default in its clothes. <!-- rule:skill-book-subject-not-cream-license --> A pinned world pins the world, not its softest rendition. <!-- rule:skill-pinned-world-not-default-rendition -->

<claude>
Your measured rendition prior: warm, bookish, family, and child-facing subjects come out as cream grounds, serif display with italic accents, and lamplight, even when the direction never asked. Before writing code, reread your OWN-WORLD block: cream, paper, parchment, ivory, or lamplight on a Persuade surface the brief did not pin means rework it from the world's saturated materials.
</claude>

## 5. Record the decision

Right after the direction choice, before any comp or code, record the chosen direction as a development-only contract under `## Direction contract` in the relevant surface brief, creating the brief if needed. Six short blocks, roughly 150 words. THESIS: the one idea this surface owns and the category default it refuses. OWN-WORLD: palette and component language, recognizable with all content removed. STORY: what the visitor understands, believes, and does. FIRST VIEWPORT: the exact composition, what is where at what scale, where the primary action sits. FORM: the chosen form, its position on your list, and the seed key. FINISH: verbatim "unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance". A block that reads like a mood means the direction is not decided. Add the approved comp's path when the comp round closes. <!-- rule:skill-decide-then-build -->

Never copy the direction contract into implementation source or any browser-delivered artifact: HTML or framework comments, hidden DOM, `<template>` elements, `data-*` attributes, rendered JSX or TSX output, serialized props or state, React Server Component payloads, client bundles, metadata or JSON-LD, accessibility-only text, or files served beside it. A compiler stripping metadata is not a safety boundary.

Use `{{scripts_path}}/impeccable surface-brief read <primary-target>` and `surface-brief write <primary-target> <body-file> [related-target ...]`; after writing, read the brief once more and verify all six contract blocks and the seed key are present. Keep the brief small: scope and mode, audience and job, chosen direction, open decisions; never PRODUCT.md truth or DESIGN.md tokens.

On a new or replacement world, the documenter writes DESIGN.md at finish from the built world (section 7); an ordinary extension does not rewrite it. <!-- rule:skill-design-md-from-the-build --> On a comp-led build the direction is visualized before it is built, never skipped; build-phase's comps phase sends you to [visualize.md](visualize.md). Code-led skips the comp round by contract, never by drift. <!-- rule:skill-visualize-before-build --> For `shape`, return the direction to [shape.md](shape.md) and stop before persistence or implementation.

## 6. Build with full commitment

Build the assigned direction, not a safer interpretation. The form supplies structure, reading order, component conventions, and native motion; the product supplies every fact. Commit every atom: nav, buttons, inputs, and links in the form's vocabulary; a stock component inside a committed form is a lapse. The first build lands fully committed; later passes clarify, never dilute. <!-- rule:skill-commit-every-atom -->

**Comp-led.** The approved comp is a measured spatial contract that only the user can downgrade, in explicit words. After the choice, send the TELEMETRY ping the roll printed, then run `{{scripts_path}}/impeccable build-phase start --direction <seed key> --artifact <entry file>` (or `--comp <approved comp>` when a surface round locked one). Close each phase with `build-phase advance` and do what its NEXT line says; exit 2 printed the reason, so fix that and advance again, and write nothing for a later phase while a gate is open. Three rules the gates assume: anything not in the spec does not exist on the page, with only three concessions (the closest obtainable font, ranked by `font-match`; icon glyphs, never a control's chrome; genuine comp defects) <!-- rule:skill-comp-spec -->; every painted region ships as a plate generated from its crop, through the shipped asset producer when subagents exist, before any page code, and the approved comp is never edited or regenerated <!-- rule:skill-plates-before-page -->; no page code before the user accepts the plan and asset review in [component-review.md](component-review.md). <!-- rule:skill-human-component-review --> The first viewport is where the run's ambition is won or lost: when the hero gate fails, open the crops it lists in order before editing, and take the route it names after three failures. <!-- rule:skill-hero-gate -->

**Code-led.** No comp and no apology; the finish reviewer audits the FIRST VIEWPORT block and signature interaction in behavior, and the chosen decision comp rides along as the critique reference.

**Both paths.** The first viewport is a thesis: demonstrate the mechanism at the scale the form has in life, never inside a standard header-and-cards shell; if a visitor would describe only a mood an hour later, it has not committed. Show the subject doing its job. Author every asset the ask round left open at production fidelity (content is authorable, claims labelable, no section omittable); gradients, glass, generic icon tiles, and many-vertex `clip-path` where an authored asset belongs are the gap wearing chrome. <!-- rule:skill-author-assets-not-chrome --> Build any technique the world names (canvas, WebGL, view transitions) for real. Pace the scroll: dense passages earn quiet ones, and the page ends on a real close. Use real, verified imagery of the subject's physical object when the brief implies it. Preserve semantics, accessibility, performance, responsiveness, conventions, and working behavior.

## 7. Inspect and finish

1. **Inspect** the target sizes in one batched round (web: desktop and mobile, plus the user's reported viewport; native: the shipped device classes, per the platform reference), fix in one batch, confirm with one more round. Two rounds is the ceiling; it never closes an open gate. On comp-led, run `{{scripts_path}}/impeccable comp-diff --comp <approved comp> --build .impeccable/review/desktop.png --spec .impeccable/build/spec.json --out-dir .impeccable/review/diff/final` and treat every region it scores missing or contradicted as a fix.
2. **Validate captures** before sending: motion settled, full-page shots from the document top, comp comparisons at the comp's pixel size, every file opened once to confirm it shows what its name claims. <!-- rule:skill-capture-validity --> Save them in `.impeccable/review/`: `desktop.png`, `mobile.png`, `user-<width>.png` on the web; one per device class on native (`phone.png`, `tablet.png`, suffixed per OS on adaptive).
3. **Review.** Spawn the shipped finish reviewer fresh, with no forked history (`impeccable-finish-reviewer`; `impeccable_finish_reviewer` with `fork_turns: 0` in codex; `/impeccable-finish-reviewer` in Cursor; "Use the impeccable-finish-reviewer agent" on GitHub Copilot). Packet: request, confirmed answers, artifact path, every screenshot path (each named required), direction contract, hook or detector findings, QUALITY BAR card and approved comp paths (the decision comp, named as critique reference, on code-led), on comp-led `.impeccable/build/state.json`, the spec, and `.impeccable/review/diff/hero/` and `diff/final/`, the craft-floor path, and on native [ios.md](ios.md) / [android.md](android.md) plus a line saying no detector ran. Do not read the agent definitions; wait with one long timeout; respawn once on an empty return. Only a harness with no subagents at all runs [degraded/finish-reviewer.md](degraded/finish-reviewer.md) in-thread, disclosed in one line. <!-- rule:skill-finish-separate-reviewer -->
4. **Act on the disposition.** recapture: new evidence, then a full review. rebuild: re-derive the named regions and assets at once and send for a full review; ask the user only on a second rebuild or when approved content would be lost. fix: one batch, rebuild, recapture the same files, and send to the same reviewer for a verdict scoring each fix (or the Verdict Pass in the degraded file). ship: continue. Two rounds ends an unattended run; attended, the user decides whether to fund another. Stop when a round resolves nothing, work only from the reviewer's list, and never run a second detector. <!-- rule:skill-verdict-bounds-the-finish -->
5. **Provenance.** A raster a fix or rebuild creates keeps its provenance per [visualize.md](visualize.md); one it abandons is deleted. Before sending, run `{{scripts_path}}/impeccable embed-prompt --scan <asset-dir...>` and embed every prompt or origin it reports missing. <!-- rule:skill-late-raster-provenance -->
6. **Report** the verdict under the reviewer's word at its real scope; a scored fix list is not whole-surface approval, and open findings are never a pass. User evidence against a ship (their screenshot, a named comp mismatch) reopens a fresh full review. <!-- rule:skill-user-evidence-reopens-review -->
7. **Document.** Spawn the shipped documenter (`impeccable-documenter`; `impeccable_documenter` in codex) with the project root, artifact path, direction contract, PRODUCT.md, [document.md](document.md), and write boundary; without subagents, load [degraded/documenter.md](degraded/documenter.md) and [document.md](document.md). New worlds need token-bearing DESIGN.md **and** `.impeccable/design.json`; extensions preserve the incumbent files and report drift without repairing it. <!-- rule:skill-documenter-records-the-world -->
8. **Close.** On comp-led, record `{{scripts_path}}/impeccable build-phase finish --disposition <word>`; a refused ship is an unfinished build, any later edit needs ship recorded again, and `fix` never ends a run over an open gate.
