# New visual work

Use this flow for a new surface or a replacement visual identity. PRODUCT.md owns product truth, DESIGN.md owns durable visual decisions, and a surface brief keeps the strategy of one route or artifact.

## 1. Decide what is already true

Read DESIGN.md, representative code, tokens, components, and assets, then name the case:

- **Redesign:** keep product truth, content, function, constraints, and explicit brand commitments; replace the old visual world rather than polishing it. The old look is evidence of what the subject is, not authority over what it becomes.
- **Established world:** inherit it, documented or not.
- **Incomplete brand:** keep confirmed assets and recognizable traits, then expand the system with the user for this surface.
- **No visual authority:** create a new world with the user.

A section, component, feature, or state inside an established surface inherits that surface. Never turn a local addition into an identity exercise.

## 2. Ask what will change the work

Before implementation, ask two or three related questions through the structured question tool; a precise request needs only a compact confirmation. Skip settled facts, never the confirmation: DESIGN.md settles the world, not this surface's purpose or concept.

- **Persuade:** who must act, what they should believe, which real proof, content, or assets earn that belief.
- **Operate:** the task, information, important states, frequency, constraints.
- **Read:** the reader's question, source material, structure, wayfinding.
- **Experience:** what leads, how exploration unfolds, which interaction or transition matters.

Across modes, ask what success looks like, what must remain untouched, and what would make a polished result feel wrong. Never ask for CSS values or canned aesthetic lanes.

## 3. Choose the right amount of invention

### Extend an existing surface

Inherit its world and composition. Resolve only the new purpose, content, hierarchy, states, interaction, and how the addition joins the surrounding experience. No concept round, and no DESIGN.md change unless the user approves a durable system change.

### Create a whole surface inside an established world

Keep the visual system fixed. Derive five to seven materially different structures from the content, task, and user behavior, ordered by resonance, then run `{{scripts_path}}/impeccable concept-seed --scope surface --mode <mode>` and follow what it prints: it deals three of your structures as equal cards and the user locks one. Anchor every card's comp on the established identity: pass a screenshot of a representative existing page as a reference image (the harness image tool's input image, or `impeccable generate-image --ref`) and lead the prompt with the new surface's structure. Locking a card is the approval: a locked comp is the approved comp, so start the build with `build-phase start --comp <that file>` and skip visualize.md's three-option round; a locked wireframe builds code-led. Never run the seed for a local extension or a precisely specified narrow request; shape those directly.

### Create or replace the visual world

1. Name the product's unique mechanism in one sentence, the audience's real scene, its cultural home, and what this first surface must prove. Name the page this category always ships and its predictable opposite: both are the rut and stay off the list. When the brief paints its own picture (a product name, a titled artifact, a governing metaphor), its literal reading joins the rut: spend at most one candidate on it.
2. From that cultural world, list seven concrete visual systems, artifacts, places, or rituals the audience knows by heart, each with one line on why it resonates and can carry the mechanism, ordered by resonance. The world includes its graphic and screen traditions: the notation, publications, identity programs, data graphics, and interfaces it reads daily; a nameable abstract system (a school of poster, a documentation standard) is as concrete as any artifact. Ask what this thing would look like as a physical object and what its world looked like before the web. On a Persuade or Experience surface, the tools and instruments the audience operates count toward the rut: at most one candidate. Near-duplicates count once; when more than three of the seven share one material family, dig until the list spans at least three families.
3. Run `{{scripts_path}}/impeccable concept-seed --scope direction --mode <mode>` and follow what it prints. No substitute, no skip: on a new or replacement world, writing artifact code before this script has run and its assignment is acknowledged is a contract violation, whatever the harness, the model, or the time pressure. The script assigns the direction to build, deals catalog challengers, and prints how to fuse, judge, and raise against them; follow it exactly. A lifted motif is a costume note, never a raise, and one world owns the page. <!-- rule:skill-concept-procedure --> <!-- rule:skill-verdict-and-donation -->
4. Present one direction, fully committed and already raised by the hands it beat, each raise a named line. Winning and competitive challengers are full alternates with their QUALITY BAR cards; declined challengers render demoted with their verdict and kept line, never silently dropped, still adoptable. A hand holds at most three full-card challengers: the rest wait in the re-roll pool, noted in one line. The IMPECCABLE'S PICK card follows the seed's rule, one at most, never in the lead, with an honest risk line naming its familiarity when true: familiar and effective is a legitimate destination, and the user deciding that trade is the point. Offer re-roll with steer and its safer and bolder registers; the register is the user's to choose. "Bolder" or "safer" said while a direction round is open means these registers, never the bolder or harden commands. <!-- rule:skill-pick-card-one-only -->

The standing exit: every direction round offers the category standard, played straight, as one quiet permanent alternative. It is the user's door, never yours: never recommend it, never weigh it against the roll, never let it soften the dealt directions. When the user takes it (the canon action, a safer steer, or plain words asking for the familiar path), convention becomes the commitment and the CANON CHOSEN line the answer prints governs; record a standing preference as a brand commitment in PRODUCT.md. <!-- rule:skill-canon-standing-exit -->

Re-roll eliminates every direction already shown; after two consecutive re-rolls, ask what quality is missing. Re-roll on your own only on named factual grounds, when the assigned direction cannot carry the product's truth or task; taste is never grounds. A user- or brief-pinned direction beats the roll, always. Resolve collisions field by field: keep every pinned constraint; in dimensions the brief leaves open, the assignment still binds through its topology, controls, state vocabulary, and ritual; when only its materials conflict with a pinned visual direction or a PRODUCT.md brand commitment, translate that material and name the translation in the presented direction. A look mismatch is not grounds to re-roll. <!-- rule:skill-assigned-plus-reroll -->

#### The decision page

concept-seed closes every roll with a PRESENTATION block; follow it. Write the options payload in the shape `{{scripts_path}}/impeccable serve-question --schema` prints: every card in the same anatomy, the assigned direction leading with its raised lines, the pick when one exists, the dealt challengers with verdicts and kept lines, re-roll with its registers, steer, `canonCard` (the category standard as one honest card), and `buildPath`. A degraded roll with no challengers still uses the page, as a single text-only card with re-roll. Run `serve-question --start --payload <file>`, open the URL it prints for the user, and collect the choice with `--wait --key <key>`, repeating while it exits 3. An ANSWER of `{"optionId":"reroll"}` keeps the page open on a loading hand: rerun concept-seed with the same `--scope` and `--mode` plus `--from <seed-key> --reroll <n>` (1 on the first re-roll, counting up), deliver the new hand with `--update --key <same key> --payload <file>`, and return to `--wait`. Never `--start` a second server or fall back to chat here: either strands the open tab. Exit 4 means the page closed unanswered: re-present once through the structured question tool, and with no answer there either, proceed with the assigned direction and state the assumptions. A harness that can leave a shell blocked in the background may run the script without `--start` and let it auto-open and block. A harness that cannot hold a blocking `--wait` (it detaches long-running commands without ever returning their output, or reaps a backgrounded child) takes the structured tool without starting the page and discloses the substitution in the first reply. A shell that hands back a still-running command as a session you can poll holds the wait: poll that session until it exits, and rerun `--wait` only after it exits without an answer. Only exit code 2 from starting it, or that wait check before starting, routes the decision to the structured tool. There the option list carries the assigned direction, the pick, the winning and competitive challengers, and the standing exit last; declined challengers fold into the assigned option as their kept lines. <!-- rule:skill-visual-decision-page -->

With image generation, every card declares a `comp` under `.impeccable/mocks/decision/`, canon included, declined challengers excepted. Serve first, then generate: the page waits per slot. `serve-question` prints a `NEXT read <path>/visualize.md` line when a round declares comps it has not generated; read that file before the first prompt, because each card's image is that direction's comp under its rules and the MODE RULES block. Produce in reading order (assigned, pick, full-card hand, canon), each file with its prompt sidecar the moment it lands, and act on any `COMP SIDECAR MISSING` or `COMP STALE` line `--wait` prints. Fairness between cards is equal fidelity in each card's own grammar, one surface, one aspect. With parallel subagents, fan out one shipped asset producer per card, up to four in flight, each with a single-comp packet: that card's fields, PRODUCT.md, the shared frame, the path to visualize.md, the COMPS section of the MODE RULES block copied verbatim (the subagent never sees the roll), and the card's declared path. Regenerate inline any slot still empty when its agent returns; drop any slot still empty when the user answers. Unchosen comps stay in `.impeccable/mocks/decision/` and imply no approval. Without image generation, cards carry their identity in palette chips and facts, and that page is complete. <!-- rule:skill-decision-comps-full-fidelity --> <!-- rule:skill-salience-parity -->

The build path, comp-led or code-led, is a workflow preference, never a round's question. The recorded default (`buildPath` in `.impeccable/config.json`, `.impeccable/config.local.json` winning; comp-led whenever image generation exists and nothing is recorded) rides every direction and surface payload as `buildPath: { "value": <default>, "toggle": true }`, and the ANSWER returns `buildPath` plus `buildPathFlipped`. A flip binds this session only, with one exception: when `buildPathFlipped` comes back true on a project that records no `buildPath` at all, ask once after the round closes whether to keep it as the standing default. Either answer writes `.impeccable/config.json`: yes writes the flipped value, "just this once" writes the value they flipped away from. Never ask when the toggle was left alone. When the user asks in words to change the default, update the file without asking. Without image generation there is no toggle and no choice: code-led, stated in one line. **Comp-led:** the chosen comp is law and non-optional, and the finish review audits the build against it. **Code-led:** no comp of this page and no apology for it; the ambition moves into the direction contract's FIRST VIEWPORT block plus a named signature interaction and motion grammar, which the finish reviewer audits in behavior. Code-led is not a discount on commitment. <!-- rule:skill-build-path-round -->

When the choice lands, open the chosen world's QUALITY BAR board and hero the ANSWER names, even if you viewed another card earlier: they set the craft level the build must reach, never the composition. A catalog world is a working system, not a mood reference: carry its system into the product as far as the MODE RULES allow.

Every direction the roll can land on must already be viable: every relationship and claim it visualizes true, a real palette and component family, a distinctive composition with one product-specific experience, workable at full-surface scale within the available assets, tools, and performance budget. A candidate that fails on truth is replaced before the roll, never rescued by it. Truth binds claims, not demonstrations: in greenfield work, author whatever illustrative material the concept needs at full fidelity, label it synthetic wherever a visitor could mistake it for the real thing, and hand the user the list of what to replace. What stays uninventable are commercial and factual claims: prices, customers, benchmarks, endpoints, capabilities the product does not have. Refusing a bold direction because its demonstration data does not exist yet is the timidity reflex wearing honesty's clothes. <!-- rule:skill-truth-binds-claims -->

`concept-seed --mode <mode>` prints the mode's MODE RULES block (DIRECTIONS and COMPS); it governs every direction, card, and comp for this surface and wins over shared guidance. A session with no roll (a direction the user or brief pinned) reads the mode's file instead: [mode-persuade.md](mode-persuade.md) for Persuade and Experience, [mode-operate.md](mode-operate.md), or [mode-read.md](mode-read.md).

## 4. Commit the world

Pick a color strategy before picking colors: Restrained (neutrals tinted from the world plus one accent), Committed (one saturated color carries 30-60% of the surface), Full palette (3-4 named roles), or Drenched (the surface IS the color). Persuade and Experience surfaces have permission for the bolder strategies; take them when the brief allows. Color commits at page scale: fields that own whole regions, not accents scattered over a neutral ground. <!-- rule:skill-color-strategy -->

Choose faces like objects from the subject's world, in the mode's register. Operate and Read surfaces are well served by system stacks and workhorse UI faces; Persuade and Experience surfaces want faces with a point of view, and these training-data defaults mean you stopped looking: Fraunces, Playfair Display, Cormorant, Lora, Crimson, Newsreader, Syne, Space Grotesk, Space Mono, IBM Plex, Inter-as-display, DM Sans, DM Serif, Outfit, Plus Jakarta Sans, Instrument Sans. Naming one anyway requires a reason no other face could satisfy, and a subject association is never that reason: books wanting a serif, bookshops wanting hand-lettering, and tech wanting a mono are the associations the list exists to break. <!-- rule:skill-typo-reflex-faces -->

Calibration: AI-generated interfaces cluster around a few looks regardless of subject: warm cream ground, high-contrast serif display, and a terracotta or signal-red accent; near-black with one neon accent and glowing edges; broadsheet-editorial hairlines, italic display serif, and small tracked mono labels; flat saturated ink with black boxes, hard offset shadows, and heavy condensed display type. All are legitimate when the brief calls for them. Where the brief leaves the aesthetic free, landing in one means the self-check failed: if someone could guess your aesthetic from the category alone, or from category-plus-avoidance, rework until neither answer is obvious. <!-- rule:skill-calibration-saturated-looks --> A brief's negative constraints (no gamification, no hype) rule out those devices, not exuberance, and adjectives describing the product's behavior (quiet support, calm coaching) do not dictate the surface's energy. <!-- rule:skill-constraints-rule-out-devices-not-energy --> A bookish, warm, or child-facing subject does not soften the calibration: book cloth, thread, jackets, endpapers, and shelf ephemera span the whole saturated spectrum, and cream plus serif for a book subject is the default wearing the subject's clothes. <!-- rule:skill-book-subject-not-cream-license --> A brief-pinned world pins the world, not its softest rendition: its full material range stays in play. <!-- rule:skill-pinned-world-not-default-rendition -->

<claude>
Your measured rendition prior: warm, bookish, family, and child-facing subjects come out as cream grounds, serif display with italic accents, and lamplight, even when the assigned direction never asked for them. Treat that first palette as already spent. Before writing code, reread your OWN-WORLD block: when it says cream, paper, parchment, ivory, or lamplight for a Persuade surface the brief did not pin, the rendition failed and you rework it from the world's saturated materials first.
</claude>

## 5. Record the decision

Right after the direction choice, before any comp or code, record the chosen direction as a development-only contract under `## Direction contract` in the relevant surface brief. A direction contract is durable route or artifact strategy, so create or update the brief even when no other surface strategy needs persistence. Use six short blocks, roughly 150 words; do not spend tool calls counting words. THESIS: the one idea this surface owns and the category-default arrangement it refuses. OWN-WORLD: the palette and component language, specific enough to be recognizable with all content removed. STORY: what the visitor understands, believes, and does. FIRST VIEWPORT: the exact composition, what is where and at what scale, and where the primary action sits. FORM: the chosen form, its position on your ordered list, and the seed key the script printed. Close with one more line, FINISH: the run's exit condition, verbatim "unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance". A page that looks complete with the FINISH line undischarged is abandoned at the finish line. If a block reads like a mood, the direction is not decided yet. Add the approved comp's path once the comp round closes. <!-- rule:skill-decide-then-build -->

Never copy the direction contract into implementation source or any browser-delivered artifact. This includes HTML or framework comments, hidden DOM, `<template>` elements, `data-*` attributes, rendered JSX or TSX output, serialized props or state, React Server Component payloads, client bundles, metadata or JSON-LD, accessibility-only text, and files served beside the artifact. A compiler or optimizer removing development metadata is not a safety boundary. Reviewers and documenters receive the contract from the surface brief.

Read the existing brief with `{{scripts_path}}/impeccable surface-brief read <primary-target>` and write it with `{{scripts_path}}/impeccable surface-brief write <primary-target> <body-file> [related-target ...]`. After writing, read the brief once more and verify that all six contract blocks and the seed key are present before building. Keep the brief small: scope and visitor mode; audience, job, action or task, proof or content, and constraints; chosen direction and memorable moment; unresolved decisions. Never copy global product truth or DESIGN.md tokens into it.

On a new or replacement world, DESIGN.md is written at finish, from the built world, by the shipped documenter (section 7); a rulebook written before the build gets defended against reality instead of describing it. A new world shipped with no DESIGN.md is still an incomplete run. An ordinary extension does not rewrite DESIGN.md. <!-- rule:skill-design-md-from-the-build -->

On a comp-led build the locked direction is visualized before it is built, never skipped: `build-phase start` opens the comps phase, and its NEXT line sends you to [visualize.md](visualize.md) for the three-option round. On a code-led build the comp round is skipped by contract, never by drift. <!-- rule:skill-visualize-before-build -->

For `shape`, return the selected direction to [shape.md](shape.md) and stop before persistence or implementation.

## 6. Build with full commitment

Build the assigned direction, not a safer interpretation of it. The form supplies structure, reading order, component conventions, and native motion; the product supplies every fact. Commit every atom: nav, buttons, inputs, and links are rebuilt in the form's vocabulary, and a stock component inside a committed form is a lapse. Land the first build fully committed; the passes that follow make the committed thing clear and effective, never dilute it. In unattended work, the safe rendition is the known risk. <!-- rule:skill-commit-every-atom -->

### Comp-led: the comp is a measured contract

An approved comp is a spatial contract, not a mood board; only the user can downgrade its authority, in explicit words. Models believe their recreation of an image succeeded when it did not, so the build runs as a state machine whose gates measure the screen against the comp. After the direction choice, send the TELEMETRY ping the roll printed, then run `{{scripts_path}}/impeccable build-phase start --direction <seed key> --artifact <entry file>`, or `start --comp <approved comp> --artifact <entry file>` when a surface round already locked one; on a recorded code-led default, start says so and stops. From there each phase closes with `{{scripts_path}}/impeccable build-phase advance`, and its NEXT line names the next phase's work: comps, spec, plates, hero, sections, motion, responsive, review. Exit 2 means the gate failed and printed why: fix that and advance again. Write nothing for a later phase while an earlier gate is open.

What the gates cannot say for you:

- **Spec.** Name every salient region by what the pixels are, splitting by what varies independently as [region-map.md](region-map.md) sets out, and inspect the map with `comp-spec --inspect-map` before producing assets. Anything not in the spec does not exist on the page: no borders, rules, containers, or chrome the comp does not show. Only three concessions exist: fonts (the closest obtainable face, ranked by `font-match`, never typed into the spec by hand), icon glyphs (the pictogram only, never a control's chrome), and genuine defects in the comp such as spelling errors. Anything drawn is a plate; icon-sized SVG is fine, and a chart the page draws from data at runtime is a chart. <!-- rule:skill-comp-spec -->
- **Plates.** Every raster region ships as a plate generated from its comp crop before any page code; the crop is only the reference, and the approved comp is never edited, composited into, or regenerated. With parallel subagents, spawn the shipped asset producer (`impeccable-asset-producer`; `impeccable_asset_producer` in codex; `/impeccable-asset-producer` in Cursor; on GitHub Copilot say "Use the impeccable-asset-producer agent") with the spec path; otherwise produce them here. A single-file deliverable inlines each plate as a data URI. `--force` exists only for the user downgrading the comp in words you quote in `--reason`. <!-- rule:skill-plates-before-page --> Then the plan and asset review in [component-review.md](component-review.md): no page code before the user accepts it. <!-- rule:skill-human-component-review -->
- **Hero.** Build only the first viewport from `build-phase scaffold`'s measured layout, plates first, the comp's words verbatim, every text region in its ranked face served from the project's own font files with `@font-face` (the capture refuses hosted font stylesheets). When the gate fails, open the region crops it lists, in order, before editing; when it names the first-viewport review, take that route instead of iterating. This is where the run's ambition is won or lost: a retry here costs minutes, a rebuild verdict at the finish costs the run. <!-- rule:skill-hero-gate -->
- **Responsive.** The first viewport must hold at common desktop widths, not only at the comp's exact size; when a region is `displaced`, restore it by resizing what sits above it.

### Code-led

No comp and no apology: the ambition lives in the direction contract's FIRST VIEWPORT block and named signature interaction, and the chosen decision comp, when one exists, rides to the finish review as the critique reference.

### Both paths

- **The first viewport is a thesis.** Demonstrate the mechanism immediately, at the scale the form has in life; never trap the concept inside a standard header-and-cards shell. If someone left after one viewport, what would they describe an hour later? If the honest answer is a mood, the concept has not committed.
- **Prove, don't claim.** Show the subject doing its job, with specifics a competitor could not copy-paste; demonstration data follows the truth rule above.
- **Author the assets; never substitute chrome.** In greenfield work every blank the ask round left open is yours to author at production fidelity: names, entries, copy, covers, thumbnails, textures. Content is authorable, claims are labelable, no section is omittable. Gradients, glass, generic icon tiles, and many-vertex `clip-path` polygons where an authored asset belongs are the gap wearing chrome. <!-- rule:skill-author-assets-not-chrome -->
- **Build the form's web leverage.** When the chosen world names a technique (canvas, WebGL, view transitions, generative motion), build the technique itself, not a static imitation.
- **Pace the scroll.** Vary density, scale, image, motion, and quiet inside one grammar; a dense passage earns a quiet one, and the page ends on a real close.
- **Use real, verified imagery when the brief implies it.** Search for the subject's physical object rather than the category; one decisive photo beats five mediocre ones. Verify stock URLs resolve.

Preserve semantics, accessibility, performance, responsiveness, project conventions, and working behavior.

## 7. Inspect and finish

Inspect the surface's target sizes in one batched screenshot round: desktop and mobile on the web; on a native platform, the shipped device classes per OS, captured as the platform reference's Verifying the build section describes. When the harness reports the user's actual viewport, add that width. Critique the render against the request and the direction contract, fix material gaps in one batch, and confirm with one final round; two rounds is the ceiling, and it never closes an open build-phase gate. On a comp-led build, run `{{scripts_path}}/impeccable comp-diff --comp <approved comp> --build .impeccable/review/desktop.png --spec .impeccable/build/spec.json --out-dir .impeccable/review/diff/final` and read its region rows and paired crops as the critique; a region it scores missing or contradicted is a fix whatever the page looks like from memory. Never judge fidelity from one full-page thumbnail.

A capture is evidence only when it is valid, and you validate before you send. Settle or disable entrance motion first: an element hidden by animation timing reads as missing and gets fixed into a regression. Capture full-page shots from the document top and the comp comparison at the comp's own pixel dimensions. Then open every file once and confirm it shows what its name claims: no black or blank regions, no wrong section behind a right filename, no half-loaded state. A malformed capture costs the whole round. <!-- rule:skill-capture-validity -->

After the second round the build thread's polishing is over: what remains ships through the handoffs. Capture the screenshots into `.impeccable/review/`, one file per inspected viewport (on the web `desktop.png` and `mobile.png`, plus `user-<width>.png` when the user's viewport joined; on native one per device class, such as `phone.png` and `tablet.png`, suffixed per OS on adaptive). A native platform runs no detector; the reviewer's floor check is its only slop gate.

Then spawn the shipped finish reviewer, `impeccable-finish-reviewer` (`impeccable_finish_reviewer` in codex; `/impeccable-finish-reviewer` in Cursor; on GitHub Copilot say "Use the impeccable-finish-reviewer agent"), fresh, with no forked history (`fork_turns: 0` in codex). Its packet: the original request, confirmed answers, the artifact path, the screenshot paths (every inspected viewport named required), the direction contract, existing hook or detector findings, the QUALITY BAR card and approved comp paths (on code-led, the chosen decision comp in that slot, named as the critique reference), on a comp-led build `.impeccable/build/state.json`, the spec, and the diff directories `.impeccable/review/diff/hero/` and `.impeccable/review/diff/final/`, the craft-floor reference path, and on a native platform [ios.md](ios.md) / [android.md](android.md) (both on adaptive) plus one line saying no detector ran. The reviewer has no browser: a screenshot you fail to pass is a check it cannot run. Never read the shipped agents' definition files; you owe only the packet. Wait with one long timeout, not a loop of short polls. Verify the return carries its five contract sections (a recapture return carries one); on an empty return, respawn once. Only a harness with no subagent capability at all runs [degraded/finish-reviewer.md](degraded/finish-reviewer.md) in-thread after stepping out of the build context, disclosed in one line at finish. <!-- rule:skill-finish-separate-reviewer -->

Act on the disposition word; there are exactly four. **recapture**: the evidence failed, not the build; recapture what it names and run a full review again, never a verdict pass. **rebuild**: fidelity failed wholesale; skip the fix batch, re-derive the named regions, produce the named assets, and send the result for a fresh full review. Tell the user what is happening rather than asking permission; consult them only on a second rebuild, both verdicts on the table, or when rebuilding would discard content they approved. **ship**: report the verdict at its scope and continue to the documenter. **fix**: apply the material fixes in one batch, rebuild once, recapture the same viewports over the same files (on a comp-led build, rerun `build-phase advance` while a phase is open), and send the recaptures to the same reviewer for a verdict scoring every fix resolved, partial, or unresolved (without agent continuation, run the Verdict Pass in [degraded/finish-reviewer.md](degraded/finish-reviewer.md)). Two rounds is the budget an unattended run ends at; in an attended session, when the second verdict still lists open items, let the user choose between shipping and funding another round. Stop the moment a round resolves nothing; the reviewer's findings are the only list you work from. Do not run a second detector. <!-- rule:skill-verdict-bounds-the-finish -->

A raster a rebuild or fix round creates or replaces keeps its **provenance** like every build raster ([visualize.md](visualize.md), Plates and provenance), and a raster the round abandons is deleted in the same batch. Before either round's result goes back for review, run `{{scripts_path}}/impeccable embed-prompt --scan <asset-dir...>` over the directories the artifact's rasters ship from and clear every file it reports by embedding its exact prompt or its origin. The scan only reads; never delete a file because it flagged it. <!-- rule:skill-late-raster-provenance -->

Report the final verdict under the reviewer's disposition word and at its actual scope: "the reviewer scored all three fixes resolved" is a claim a verdict pass supports, "no material issues remain" is not. Never announce a table with open material findings as a pass. When the user answers a ship with evidence against it (their own screenshot, a named mismatch with the comp), that evidence outranks every capture you made: put it in the packet and spawn a fresh reviewer for a new full review. <!-- rule:skill-user-evidence-reopens-review -->

After the last correction, spawn the shipped documenter, `impeccable-documenter` (`impeccable_documenter` in codex), with the project root, artifact path, direction contract, PRODUCT.md, [document.md](document.md), and write boundary. Without subagents, load [degraded/documenter.md](degraded/documenter.md) and [document.md](document.md) before writing. New worlds and approved system changes require token-bearing DESIGN.md **and** `.impeccable/design.json`, not prose alone. Ordinary extensions compare the build against the incumbent system, preserve its files, and report the evidence checked; report pre-existing drift without repairing it unasked. Finish only when review and documentation are complete. <!-- rule:skill-documenter-records-the-world -->

On a comp-led build, record the reviewer's disposition with `{{scripts_path}}/impeccable build-phase finish --disposition <ship|fix|rebuild|recapture>` before the final response. A refused `ship` is an unfinished build: report the outstanding phase with the verdict. Any edit after the last captured gate voids that capture: run `finish --disposition ship` again before you stop. Never record `fix` to end a run over an open gate; take the route the gate names, and when the user leaves it open, report that phase as open.
