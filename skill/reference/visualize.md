# Visualize: Direction Comps & Asset Production

Read this before writing any comp prompt: the decision comps of a direction or surface round (serve-question's NEXT line sends you here) and the comp round of a comp-led build (build-phase's comps phase sends you here). The MODE RULES block concept-seed printed for this surface (COMPS) governs these comps together with this file and wins where they conflict; with no roll this session, read the mode's file named in new-work.md. The visual world is already decided, by DESIGN.md or, on a new world, by the chosen direction: a comp tests composition, hierarchy, density, and focal moment, never palette, type voice, material, or motion. If the world cannot support the concept, return to the concept shortlist rather than changing the world.

## Every comp

- **Frame.** The requested surface at its own viewport: portrait at device size for a native app or mobile-first surface, desktop landscape otherwise. Render the page itself edge to edge, never a browser window, device, or desk around it.
- **Structure leads.** A comp is a designed surface, not a picture of the subject. Lead the prompt with the surface's own regions, named in order with their scale relationships; a page with no navigation says so, and an unconventional surface states its skeleton. Atmosphere first gets a vignette back: the fish market instead of the fish market's website. If a render could hang as a poster, or reads as a photograph with some text on it, regenerate with the regions named and their parts working in place.
- **The subject is present.** The subject appears as the content the regions hold; the world dresses the frame and never displaces what it shows. Exclusion lists bind claims, never the subject. Before accepting a render, point at the subject; if it depicts everything about the world and nothing of the subject, regenerate with the subject's content named region by region.
- **The job reads.** Name the surface's mode from the render with no caption; if you cannot, regenerate with the visitor's job as the prompt's spine.
- **Depth, not spread.** The world enters through one dominant move plus the material, type, and spacing that support it; the other regions hold still in that material so the move can be read. The page still carries its content (a way to navigate, the offer or task, supporting copy, an action, a glimpse of what follows) at real reading size, body text near 16px on a 1440px-wide page; where each piece sits is the composition's call. A second element competing with the focal moment at the same scale means the comp is shouting: regenerate keeping the strongest move and quieting the rest. Busy is louder, not bolder.
- **Real material.** Real product name and real content; no invented commercial claims. Generated imagery is a material, not a claim.
- **Established world.** Pass a screenshot of a representative existing page as a reference image (`impeccable generate-image --ref`, or the harness tool's input image) and lead the prompt with the new surface's structure. Chrome, palette, type, and component character carry over; the reference page's content does not.
- **Record it.** Every comp is saved with its prompt sidecar (`a.png` gets `a.png.json`; `generate-image` writes it itself, a harness tool does not). Open images by workspace-relative path; sandboxed viewers reject absolute ones.

## The comp round

`build-phase start` runs before the first comp of this round; a harness image tool is bound by the same order `generate-image` enforces. Render three high-fidelity comps of the requested surface under `.impeccable/mocks/`: one comp invites rubber-stamping. The chosen card's decision comp is the first of the three: copy it and its sidecar unchanged into `.impeccable/mocks/` (the gate counts files there) and never regenerate it. Generate two more from the direction in words, never with the first comp as an input image (a reference image anchors the layout and the three collapse into one). How they vary from the first is the mode's call, in its COMPS rules; a variant that tidies the first adds no choice. With no decision comp, render all three here. Comp-round comps are the build thread's own work, never delegated: that thread must have seen every comp when the build starts.

A surface-scope round that already put visualized cards before the user has discharged this round: the locked card's comp is the approved comp. Record the approval as below and generate nothing new.

## One approval point

Show the three together on the decision page (`impeccable serve-question`, one option per comp with the comp as its image), or in the harness only when it renders images inline; a text-only surface does not count as display. Ask what should carry forward, what feels false to the world, and whether to approve, combine, revise, or reject. Then stop and wait. A structured simulated user counts as attended and receives the same question.

Do not begin code until the user approves a comp or explicitly delegates the choice. This approval point has no substitute and no skip condition: when the decision page cannot start, ask through the structured question tool; only after both fail may you treat the choice as delegated. A delegated pick is chosen from the brief and PRODUCT.md, recorded exactly as an approval, and disclosed in your first reply. The finish reviewer treats comp-round comps with no recorded approval as a material finding; decision comps under `.impeccable/mocks/decision/` imply no approval on their own.

After approval, the approved comp's path goes in the surface brief and its sidecar gains `"approved": true`, which is what `build-phase advance` reads to close the comps phase. Summarize the composition and the parts of the comp that must not be literalized, then continue the phased build in new-work.md section 6.

## After approval: the comp becomes a spec

The approved comp is a north star for translation into semantic, responsive, accessible code, never a license to recompose: keeping the palette and mood while redrawing the topology is a second art direction. Do not rasterize core UI text or controls, and do not substitute a different visual driver without asking. The medium of every region follows from what the pixels are, never from what feels buildable: figures, product objects, any illustration with perspective, shading, or drawing skill, and any named texture (cloth, paper grain, leather, brushed metal) are `plate`, `image`, or `texture` regions and ship as rasters; text, controls, chrome, diagrams with countable elements, flat shape systems, and anything that must move or respond are semantic. Writing "CSS" for a sculpted panel's finish, or a many-vertex `clip-path` for a torn edge, quietly deletes the approved design. Dropping an image-native region is a scope decision the user makes at the approval point, never a silent flattening after it.

## Plates and provenance

The plates phase's NEXT line carries the production commands. Generation context is part of the asset: after generating any image with any tool, run `{{scripts_path}}/impeccable embed-prompt <image> --prompt "<prompt>"` with the exact string the tool received (`impeccable generate-image` does this itself); `--read` recovers it and `--scan <dir>` lists rasters still missing one. The embedded prompt plus the region's row in the spec is the raster's **provenance**, and every raster the artifact references carries it; a sourced, stock, or pre-existing raster embeds its origin instead. A raster created or replaced later, in a fix batch or a rebuild, is produced the same way; a raster a fix abandons is deleted in the same batch.
